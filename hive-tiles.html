How to play. You share two number pads. On your turn, change exactly one pad to resize your rectangle (the other side stays as your opponent left it), optionally Rotate it, then move it over the board and click / tap to drop it. It must sit on empty squares, fully inside the grid — you can't cover a piece that's already down. The pads then pass to your opponent. Play until no more pieces fit anywhere; whoever has covered the most squares wins.
