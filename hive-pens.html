Bee Keeper

area · strategy
🐝 Build fences to pen off the meadow. Close a shape and it fills with bees — one bee per triangle. Most bees wins!
Opponent
Player 10
Player 20
Player 1 — draw a fence between two dots.
How to play. Take turns drawing one fence between two neighbouring dots. When your fence closes off an area — any shape — every triangle inside becomes yours and fills with bees (a triangle is 1 bee, a rhombus 2, and so on). Turns always alternate, and once a pen has bees it's sealed — no more fences inside. When no fences can be added, whoever has the most bees wins.
