Press spin to get your first digit.
How to play. Each spin gives you a digit 0–9. Tap an empty box to drop it in — but once it's placed, it's stuck. Fill every box to make the biggest number you can (or smallest, if you switch). The trick: do you gamble a big place on an early digit, or play it safe? At the end we rank your number against every arrangement of the digits you drew.
