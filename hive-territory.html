How to play. Take turns planting a seed on any empty hex. Each hex is then coloured for whoever's seeds have the most influence over it. A seed's influence = how factorable its number is (shown as dots: 24 has 6 factor pairs → strong; 11 only 2 → weak). Power spreads in rings and drops by one each hex outward, so a strength-6 seed reaches six rings while a weak one barely holds its own patch. When all seeds are planted, whoever colours the most hexes wins.
