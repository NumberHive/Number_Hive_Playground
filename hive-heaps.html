How to play. The number is broken into prime blocks — 360 = 2×2×2 · 3×3 · 5, so a stack of three 2s, two 3s and one 5. On your turn, pick one prime stack and take away as many blocks as you like from it (that's dividing the number by that prime). Tap a block to remove it and everything above it. Whoever takes the very last block — reducing the number to 1 — wins. Tip: you're really playing a game of Nim on the prime powers.
