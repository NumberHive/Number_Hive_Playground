How to play. Choose a brick height with − / +, then drag bricks from the tray onto each tower (or tap a tower). If a brick fills a tower to the top exactly, that height divides it. When one brick height fills both towers exactly it's a common factor — try taller ones. The tallest that fills both is the highest common factor. Overshoot the top and you've got a leftover.
