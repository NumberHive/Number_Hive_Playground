How to play. Start on the amber hex (bottom right); your two factor pads multiply to its value. A move changes exactly one pad — the new product must match a hex touching the one you're on. Reach the green goal (top left). You can wander freely, revisit hexes and take wrong turns — every board is built so the goal always stays reachable, so you can never get permanently stuck. Undo takes back a step; the counter rewards a short route. Solve a maze to unlock the next. There are 100, getting harder as you climb.
