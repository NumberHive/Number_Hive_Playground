Top Cell

each cell is the product of the two below · find the top cell to win
You found the top cell! 🍯
How to play. Every hex equals the two hexes below it multiplied together. Some cells are filled in. Work out the blanks — multiply two cells to get the one above, or divide when you know a cell and one of the two beneath it — until you fill the whole pyramid and reveal the top cell. Tap a blank, type the number, then press ✓.
