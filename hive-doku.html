Hive-doku

every row & column uses each number once · cages must hit their target
Solved! 🎉
How to play. Fill every hex so each row and each column contains the numbers 1 to N exactly once (like Sudoku). The coloured groups are cages: the little label shows a target and a sign — × means the cage's numbers must multiply to it, + means they add to it. The plain grey hexes are given clues. Tap a hex, then tap a number.
