Goal: find the prime factors. Tap a number, then tap one of its factors to split it into two branches (split 60 into 2 and 30). Keep splitting the amber numbers until every branch is a green prime — those are the answer. On Easy the buttons show you which factors work; on Medium and Hard you have to pick the right factor yourself, and a wrong pick costs a move. Try to match the fewest possible moves shown up top. Stuck? Hint splits one for you.
