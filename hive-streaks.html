How to play. Two factor pads are shared. On your turn, change exactly one pad — the product points to a cell, which you claim for your colour if it's free. The pads then pass to your opponent, who changes one of them, and so on.

Scoring. Each turn you bank points for the streaks you just made. A run of n cells in a straight line is worth n! — a lone cell is 1, two is 2, three is 6, four is 24, five is 120 — and it all banks and only adds up. Streak multiplier: if one move makes more than one streak at once, your points for that turn are multiplied by how many. Two 2-runs together = (2 + 2) × 2 = 8; a 3-run plus a 2-run = (6 + 2) × 2 = 16. First to 201 wins.
